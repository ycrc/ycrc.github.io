    Apply For an AICR Project

    To request access to AICR, prepare a Project Description (described below) of no more than two (2) pages in PDF format.

    AICR Project Allocations. Approved projects are granted access for 12 months, at which point they must reapply and be accepted to retain access. Equitable resource allocations both between Yale projects and with the other institutions is governed through QOS limits and fairshare rather than compute hour allocations to allow for flexible use of the resources. Each project is granted a default 1TiB work storage allocation in addition to individual user home and scratch space (additional storage may be requested if justified in your proposal). Each project is limited to 4 users.

    If a project is not renewed (either not accepted or not resubmitted), the team must retrieve their data from AICR within 30 days, at which point all remaining data will be deleted from AICR and project team members’ access will be deactivated to release resources for incoming projects.

    Review Process. AICR requests are reviewed in two stages. YCRC first evaluates whether the computational resources requested are appropriate and justified and the PI team has sufficient experience and expertise to execute the proposed work. Proposals approved in that stage proceed to an independent faculty panel that assesses research merit and impact. Make sure your research case and your need for AICR are clear to both audiences.

    Eligibility

    Applications must be led by a full-time Yale faculty or staff member who is eligible to serve as a Principal Investigator (PI) for external grants.

    One Submission per Call: A PI may submit one proposal per 6-month application cycle for a 12-month project allocation. Each proposal should describe a specific research project with clearly defined goals and computational resource needs.

    Applicants must identify up to four team members who will require access to AICR and briefly describe each person's role in the proposed project (see Section C). Changes to authorized personnel may be requested during the allocation period.

    AICR may be used only with data classified as low risk. Projects involving moderate- or high-risk data, controlled datasets, protected health information (PHI), or personally identifiable information (PII) are not eligible.

    Prepare Your Proposal

    Submission Checklist:

    • Applications should be submitted through the Yale Infoready portal. \<LINK>
    • Project Description (2 pages maximum, PDF)
    • References (optional)
    • CV (optional)

    Project Description

    Format: 2 page max, PDF [single-spaced; font: 11pt or larger]

    To ensure your request can be properly reviewed, your description should include the sections outlined here. See the included AICR Project Description Guide, which may help you prepare a high quality project description.

    A. Research Goals

    A faculty panel will review this section for research merit, feasibility and impact. In roughly half a page to one page, address the following:

    • Question and significance. What question or problem will the project address? Why does it matter, and what gap will the work fill?
    • Computational approach. Explain your computational approach and how it will address the research question.
    • Expected outcomes. What concrete results or milestones do you expect to reach during the 12-month project allocation? What is the broader impact of this research?

    Write for faculty reviewers outside your subfield.

    B. Resources (Compute and Storage)

    YCRC uses this section to assess whether the requested resources fit the proposed work. In about one page, address the following. Be specific as possible in your proposal along with data supporting the resources made. A short table is welcome if it makes the estimates easier to read.

    • Workload and fit for AICR. Briefly describe what you plan to run. Identify the GPU type you expect to use (B200, RTX 6000, or both) and explain why the workload needs those resources.
    • Compute estimate. Estimate the total GPU-hours needed over the allocation period, by GPU type if you expect to use both. For each main workload, give a representative job size and runtime, and estimate the maximum number of GPUs you expect to use at once. Show how you arrived at the totals using prior runs, benchmarks, or published results. Give approximate run counts or ranges where they help explain the estimate.
    • Storage. If you need more than the default 1 TiB of work storage, estimate the total in TiB and explain why. Describe where your data will be stored after the end of the project allocation.
    • Software and data. Describe any Data Use Agreements or access restrictions involving the data in your project (noting that AICR is only approved for low-risk data). Note any required software, licensing needs, or public datasets relevant to running the work.
    • If compute resources are constrained, briefly indicate which work you would prioritize or scale back.

    C. Team and Preparedness

    Describe your team's qualifications and readiness to execute the project both in using the methods proposed and the resources requested.

    • What systems have you recently used and what was the scale of the AI applications?
    • Describe prior work on the proposed computation on other systems, such as the YCRC’s Bouchet. If you described prior runs in Section B, refer to them rather than repeating the details.
    • List the team members who need accounts to work on the project. AICR project teams are limited to four members. Briefly describe each person's role in the proposed project.

    Supporting Documents

    A CV and a list of references are optional. If included, submit them separately from the Project Description. They do not count toward its two-page limit.

    How to Submit Your Proposal

    AICR Project Proposals should be submitted to Yale InfoReady Platform. Click the "Apply for AICR" button below to access the Platform and Submission Forms.

    Apply to AICR (Due Dec 10, 2026)

    If you have any questions about AICR or the proposal process, contact the YCRC at research.computing@yale.edu
